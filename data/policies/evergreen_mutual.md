## EGM-DIAGNOSIS Diagnosis requirements
A primary ICD-10-CM diagnosis code is required on every psychotherapy claim.

## EGM-TIME-CODES Psychotherapy time-based codes
Psychotherapy codes follow the standard time ranges: 90832 for 16 to 37 minutes, 90834 for 38 to 52 minutes and 90837 for 53 minutes or more. The start and stop times must appear in the session note.

## EGM-90837-AUTH Extended sessions need prior authorization
Every session billed as 90837 requires a prior authorization number on the claim, starting with the first such session. Sessions billed as 90832 or 90834 do not need prior authorization. Claims for 90837 without a prior authorization number are denied.

## EGM-TELEHEALTH Telehealth by live video
Evergreen Mutual does not accept modifier 95. Append modifier GT to every psychotherapy session delivered by real-time audio and video, and report place of service 02 for all telehealth regardless of where the patient is located. Video visits billed with modifier 95 instead of GT are denied.

## EGM-AUDIO-ONLY Audio-only (telephone) sessions
Psychotherapy delivered by telephone without video is not a covered service under Evergreen Mutual plans. Audio-only sessions will be denied whatever codes or modifiers are billed. Providers should convert the session to video or collect payment under the member's non-covered services agreement.

## EGM-IN-OFFICE In-office visits
Report place of service 11 for sessions held in the provider's office. No modifier is needed.

## EGM-TIMELY-FILING Timely filing
Submit claims within 90 days of the date of service.
