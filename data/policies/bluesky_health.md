## BSH-DIAGNOSIS Diagnosis requirements
Every outpatient psychotherapy claim must include at least one ICD-10-CM diagnosis code that supports medical necessity. Claims with no diagnosis code are returned unprocessed.

## BSH-TIME-CODES Psychotherapy time-based codes
Bill psychotherapy by the face-to-face time documented in the session note. Use 90832 for 16 to 37 minutes, 90834 for 38 to 52 minutes and 90837 for 53 minutes or more. Sessions shorter than 16 minutes are not billable. If the documented time and the billed code disagree, the claim is denied and must be corrected and resubmitted.

## BSH-TELEHEALTH Telehealth by live video
Psychotherapy delivered by real-time audio and video is covered at the same rate as in-office care. Append modifier 95 to every video visit. Report place of service 10 when the patient joins from their home, and place of service 02 when the patient joins from any other location. Video visits billed without modifier 95 are denied.

## BSH-AUDIO-ONLY Audio-only (telephone) sessions
Psychotherapy by telephone without video is covered for established patients. Append modifier 93 instead of modifier 95, and report the same place of service rules as video visits. The session note must state why video was not used.

## BSH-PRIOR-AUTH Session limits and prior authorization
The first 20 outpatient psychotherapy sessions per member per calendar year do not need prior authorization. From the 21st session onward, a prior authorization number must be on the claim. Claims for session 21 or later without a prior authorization number are denied.

## BSH-IN-OFFICE In-office visits
Report place of service 11 for sessions held in the provider's office. No modifier is needed for in-office psychotherapy.

## BSH-TIMELY-FILING Timely filing
Submit claims within 180 days of the date of service. Corrected claims must be submitted within 180 days of the original denial.
