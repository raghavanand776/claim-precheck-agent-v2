## CCP-DIAGNOSIS Diagnosis requirements
Claims must include an ICD-10-CM diagnosis code. Z codes may be reported as secondary diagnoses only.

## CCP-TIME-CODES Psychotherapy time-based codes
Use 90832 for 16 to 37 minutes, 90834 for 38 to 52 minutes and 90837 for 53 minutes or more of psychotherapy.

## CCP-PRIOR-AUTH Session limits and prior authorization
Cascade Care covers 12 outpatient psychotherapy sessions per member per calendar year without prior authorization. Session 13 and every session after it require a prior authorization number on the claim. Request authorization through the provider portal before the 13th session; authorizations cannot be issued after the date of service.

## CCP-TELEHEALTH Telehealth by live video
Append modifier 95 to video psychotherapy. Report place of service 10 when the patient is at home and place of service 02 otherwise.

## CCP-AUDIO-ONLY Audio-only (telephone) sessions
Audio-only psychotherapy is covered with modifier 93 when the member does not have access to video.

## CCP-IN-OFFICE In-office visits
Report place of service 11 for sessions held in the provider's office.

## CCP-TIMELY-FILING Timely filing
Submit claims within 365 days of the date of service.
